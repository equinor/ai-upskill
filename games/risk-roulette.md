# Risk Roulette

## Get the game

The game was originally developed as a physical card game, see [the PDF in this directory](./risk-roulette.pdf), and I think this is still the best way to play it.

It was then ported to a small standalone web app, see [this repo](https://github.com/equinor/ai-risk-roulette), now archived.

The game is now one of the pages in my project [`figgy`](https://github.com/equinor/figgy).

## How to play

- Shuffle the cards and distribute 5–10 cards to each player/group.
- The task is to sort the ideas into "Probably okay" and "Probably risky"; alternatively, sort from low to high risk.
- Allow about one minute per card.
- Option: Ask players to share cards from their categories.
- Reveal the rubric. Ask players to share more, especially those ranked differently to the rubric.

## Some principles for safe AI

- Consequences of errors are small.
- You have expertise in the topic or domain of your query.
- AI is assisting (eg highlighting errors in a translation) not executing (eg fixing the errors).
- If a person is the subject of your query, you have express permission from them to use AI.
- On large-scale tasks, the average performance is more important than each example being correct.
